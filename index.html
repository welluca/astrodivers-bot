AstroDivers Stream Bot

The chat bot of the AstroDivers astrophotography live stream.

AstroDivers streams live images from its telescopes. The bot takes part in the stream's own YouTube live chat:

  • it finds the stream's current live broadcast;
  • it reads the chat, so viewers can answer trivia questions, vote for the next target and ask questions about the sky;
  • it posts short replies in the chat.

It works only on the AstroDivers channel's own streams. Viewers never sign in to it.

The bot uses YouTube API Services. By taking part through YouTube chat you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service.
