Privacy policy

AstroDivers Stream Bot · last updated 8 October 2026

AstroDivers Stream Bot is the chat bot of the AstroDivers live stream. It uses YouTube API Services. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Who signs in

Only the AstroDivers YouTube channel authorizes the bot, with its own Google account. It lets the bot see the channel's own live broadcasts, read their live chat and post in it. Viewers never sign in to the bot and never give it access to their accounts.

What the bot receives

When you write in the live chat of an AstroDivers stream, the bot receives what YouTube shows everyone in that chat:

  • your display name and your channel ID;
  • the text of your message;
  • for Super Chats, Super Stickers, memberships and gifts: the type and the amount.

It receives nothing else about you, and nothing from your device.

How it is used

  • The stream's games: trivia answers and scores, votes for the next target, and questions for the stream's astronomy assistant (!ask).
  • On the stream: your display name and message can appear on screen, for example in the chat feed or the trivia standings. A question you ask, or a target you look up, can be read aloud with your display name.
  • Replies: the bot can answer you in the chat.

What is kept, and for how long

  • Chat messages are held in the server's memory to show the chat feed. They are not written to disk.
  • Saved: trivia scores, with your display name; questions you ask with !ask, with your display name and the answer (the last 200 only). Votes are saved as totals, without names.
  • The server's logs can contain display names and commands. They are overwritten after about two weeks.
  • Your display name and channel ID are kept no longer than 30 days after your last message. Then they are deleted, or replaced with an anonymous label; points and totals stay.

Who else receives it

Nothing is sold or used for advertising. To run the stream, two services process some of it:

  • Anthropic (the Claude API): the text of a question put to the astronomy assistant, and for questions collected together, the asker's display name, to write an answer.
  • Microsoft (text-to-speech): the lines the stream reads aloud, which can include a display name, to turn them into speech.

Messages the bot posts go through YouTube.

Cookies and advertising

The bot places no cookies and reads nothing from your device. These pages set no cookies either. They are hosted by GitHub, which may log visits under the GitHub Privacy Statement. The bot shows no advertising. YouTube may show ads on the stream under its own terms.

Your choices

  • To have your display name and channel ID deleted sooner, email astrodivers@gmail.com. It is done within 7 days.
  • Anyone who has given an app access to a Google account can remove it on the Google security settings page.

Contact

Questions or complaints about these practices: astrodivers@gmail.com.
